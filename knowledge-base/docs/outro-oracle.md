## messageOracle

Our system has integrated different oracles to provide highly accurate price feeds for our users.

River protects price accuracy by checking price staleness. The oracle updates its price when the value deviates beyond a specified threshold or when the heartbeat idle time has passed. Our protocol checks the timestamp and verifies that the latest answer is recent enough.
