## River4FUN

Connect your X, stake tokens to earn extra reward with zero cost.

River4FUN turns your contributions ( tweets and social engagement ) into River Pts you can use for project airdrops and governance rewards.

By linking social engagement with on-chain incentives, River4FUN drives real value for both users and projects.

Linking your X handle unlocks your initial River Pts airdrop

-   Navigate to 4FUN in the River app
    
-   Connect your X account
    
-   Tweet & Tag @River4FUN
    

**Benefit for you:** claim free River Pts and enable all features

**Benefit for projects:** gain verified support and amplify reach

Staking earns River Pts daily with zero lock-up

-   Navigate to 4FUN
    
-   **Stake** in any token supported ( USD1, wBNB and 20+ tokens on bnb chain )
    
-   Enter an amount, approve the transaction
    

Benefit for you: earn extra rewards every day without locking your assets

Benefit for projects**:** secure committed liquidity and signal strong holder confidence

See staking in action: [https://youtu.be/jNgoKh39cag?si=iJ30QcV\_cZ6Lo8dCarrow-up-right](https://youtu.be/jNgoKh39cag?si=iJ30QcV_cZ6Lo8dC)

Claim & Stkaed River Pts to boost your reward.

Staked River Pts count as votes (1 Pt = 1 vote). You can cast votes every 24 hours to list or boost projects and earn campaign rewards.

-   Navigate to 4FUN
    
-   Scroll down and switch to "Vote" tab
    
-   select a project, click vote
    

**Benefit for you:** earn campaign rewards and influence protocol decisions

**Benefit for projects:** obtain fair, data-driven community endorsements
