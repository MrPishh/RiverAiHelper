## Omni CDP

Mint / Swap for satUSD to access liquidity across ecosystem.

**Omni-CDP** is an omni-chain liquidity management system that lets you deposit BTC, ETH, or LSTs on one chain and mint satUSD directly on another, enabling seamless capital deployment across ecosystems.

-   [Mint satUSD](https://docs.riverai.inc/how-to-use/omni-cdp/mint-satusd) — Deposit BTC, ETH, BNB, or LSTs as collateral, mint satUSD instantly on your destination chain.
    
-   [Swap satUSD](https://docs.riverai.inc/how-to-use/omni-cdp/swap-satusd) — Swap satUSD with USDT, USDC, and other stablecoins at a 1:1 ratio via built-in stable pools.
    
-   [Stake satUSD](https://docs.riverai.inc/how-to-use/omni-cdp/stake-satusd)— Stake satUSD and receive satUSD+ to earn protocol revenue.
    
-   [Bridge satUSD](https://docs.riverai.inc/how-to-use/omni-cdp/bridge-satusd) — Powered by LayerZero, move satUSD across chains natively with no third-party bridges, no slippage, and no wait.
