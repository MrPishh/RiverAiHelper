## Swap satUSD

Exchange satUSD at a fixed 1:1 ratio for USDT, USDC, or USD1 using River’s swap interface.

Tap into deep USDT pools on PancakeSwap or any integrated DEX, then redeploy your assets into lending, farming, or cross-chain yield strategies—all in a few clicks.
