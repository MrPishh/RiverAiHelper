# Governance: The Countermeasure 1.3 : Buyback Execution
Source: https://gov.river.inc/t/the-countermeasure-1-3-buyback-execution/13
Scraped: 2026-05-10T01:04:26.943Z

---

Following [The Countermeasure 1.2 : Buyback List Update](https://gov.river.inc/t/the-countermeasure-1-2-buyback-list-update/11), River has officially entered the Public Sale Buyback Execution Phase.

This plan applies only to Public Sale participants who still hold their River Pts as of the snapshot, and aims to repurchase eligible tokens in BNB at a fixed rate.

It is a procedural action for ecosystem stability — not a new investment or yield event.

### [](#p-16-buyback-details-1)✦ Buyback Details

All eligible address must, before 2025-11-20 13:00 UTC, send the exact number of River Pts recorded in the snapshot (2025-11-09 11:09 UTC) to the one and only official buyback address below:

```
0x076dd5851495a3ac12f0101d3e2107462890d9cf
```

![:warning:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/warning.png?v=15 ":warning:") This is the only valid and permanent address.

River will never publish another address, send private messages, or ask users to transfer through intermediaries.

Any other address or message claiming to represent River is a scam.

After the buyback window closes, the team will verify all on-chain transactions and distribute BNB based on verified amounts.

### [](#p-16-how-to-proceed-2)✦ How to Proceed

*   Check your eligible amount: [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JldFVvP-7IccVVae5as21bv6BSTnc1hgabZQY0E\_Clc/edit?usp=sharing](https://t.co/hWlC3xKIqg)
    
*   Send the same amount of River Pts from your Public Sale wallet to the official buyback address above before the deadline.
    
*   The River team will verify all transactions and complete BNB distribution by 2025-11-23.
    

![⚠️|20.3984375x20.3984375](https://canada1.discourse-cdn.com/flex008/uploads/river1/original/1X/2263bddf34ab900cc31de8de7f2cedff7afcd8c9.svg "Warning sign") Transactions sent to any incorrect or unofficial address are non-refundable and cannot be recovered.

### [](#p-16-important-notes-3)✦ Important Notes

*   Eligible only for River Pts purchased during the Public Sale and still held (staked, untransferred, unconverted) at the snapshot.
    
*   River Pts acquired from secondary markets or transferred before the snapshot are not eligible for buyback.
    
*   Any non-eligible address sending River Pts will be treated as invalid and not included in the buyback.
    
*   Invalid transactions will not be refunded.
