## [](#p-13-river-pts-public-sale-buyback-plan-1)River Pts Public Sale Buyback Plan

Due to the temporary suspension of River Pts conversions,  
We understand that many supporters have felt anxious and uncertain during this period.

To address community feedback and protect the interests of Public Sale participants, the team has initiated a full buyback plan and finalized the snapshot time and detailed process

### [](#p-13-buyback-details-2)✦ Buyback Details

*   Buyback Price : Final Price = 0.0000447 BNB (≈ $0.048 USD)
    
*   Snapshot Time : November 9, 2025 at 11:09 UTC — the exact time when River Pts conversions were paused
    
*   Snapshot Scope : Each Public Sale participant’s wallet balance of River Pts at the snapshot Time.
    
*   Buyback Eligibility : Only River Pts purchased during the Public Sale and, as of the snapshot, still held (including staked River Pts) and not sold, transferred, or converted are eligible.
    
*   Calculation Method :  
    Actual Buyback Amount = Public Sale Purchased Amount − Transferred / Sold / Converted Amount
    

In short, the team will buy back the same amount of River Pts that each participant still held (including staked Pts) at the snapshot.

River Pts acquired from secondary markets, transferred out, or already converted are not included.

The final buyback amount will be determined based on the actual holdings at the snapshot.

### [](#p-13-buyback-timeline-3)✦ Buyback Timeline

*   Nov 10 | Public Sale buyback plan announced
    
*   Nov 12 – Nov 20 | Eligible addresses and amounts published; participants send River Pts to the designated buyback address
    
*   Nov 20 – Nov 23 | Team reviews and confirms all buyback addresses and amounts
    
*   Nov 23 | River team completes all BNB buybacks and transfers to eligible addresses
    

### [](#p-13-important-notes-4)✦ Important Notes

*   River Pts sent from non–Public Sale addresses will be treated as burned and not eligible for buyback.
    
*   Do not send more than your original purchased or eligible amount; any excess will neither be bought back nor returned.
    
*   Please double-check the address and River Pts amount before transferring to ensure successful completion of the buyback.

## [](#p-13-river-pts-public-sale-5)\[ 應對措施第一步 : 回購計劃 \] River Pts Public Sale 回購方案

因 River Pts 轉換暫時停止，我們深知這段時間讓許多支持者感到焦慮與不安

為回應社群建議並保障 Public Sale 參與者權益，已啟動 全額 BNB 回購計劃，並確定了具體快照時間與流程

### [](#p-13-h-6)✦ 回購資訊

*   回購價格：Final price = 0.0000447 BNB（~$0.048 USD）
    
*   快照時間：11 月 9 日 11 點 09 分 ( UTC ) 暫停轉換的時間
    
*   統計範圍：每個參與 Public Sale 的錢包在該快照時間下的 River Pts 餘額
    
*   回購範圍：僅針對 Public Sale 時購買、且在快照時間前始未售出、未轉出、未轉換或仍在質押中的 River Pts 數量
    
*   計算方式：實際回購數量 = Public Sale 認購量 − 已轉出/已售出/已轉換數量
    

Public Sale 參與者在快照時間下，仍持有多少 River Pts，就按該數量回購  
不包含二級市場購得或已轉出、已轉換之 River Pts 數量。

以快照當下該地址的 River Pts 實際持有量（含質押中）為最終依據。

### [](#p-13-river-pts-public-sale-7)✦ River Pts Public Sale 回購時間表

Public Sale 參與地址、符合快照資格者，團隊將按 = 0.0000447 BNB 全額 BNB 回購

*   11 月 10 日 | Public Sale 回購方案公告及統計
    
*   11 月 12 日 ~ 11 月 20 日 | 公告 Public Sale 回購名單和數量，將 River Pts 轉至指定地址
    
*   11 月 20 日 ~ 11 月 23 日 | 團隊確認所有回購地址、數量
    
*   11 月 23 日 | 團隊完成 River Pts 回購，發放 BNB 至所有地址
    

### [](#p-13-h-8)✦ 注意事項

*   非 Public Sale 地址的 River Pts 轉回該地址，將視為銷毀處理，無法退還
    
*   請勿打回超過原認購數量、符合快照資格的數量，任何超額部分不予退還
    
*   轉 River Pts 前請再次核對地址與數量，以確保回購順利完成
