## [](#p-14-river-pts-buyback-list-update-1)River Pts Buyback List Update

Following The Countermeasure 1: The Buyback, we’re moving forward with the next step of the plan.

Based on the snapshot taken at 11:09 UTC, Nov 9, the qualified Public Sale addresses and amounts have been finalized.

All eligible participants must send the same amount of River Pts recorded in the snapshot to the official buyback address (to be announced tomorrow) before Nov 12, 1:00 PM UTC.

After the buyback period ends, the team will verify all transactions and distribute BNB buyback at 0.0000447 BNB per River Pts according to verified amounts.

Public Sale Buyback List : [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JldFVvP-7IccVVae5as21bv6BSTnc1hgabZQY0E\_Clc/edit?usp=sharing](https://t.co/hWlC3xKIqg)

### [](#p-14-important-notes-2)✦ Important Notes

*   The buyback applies only to River Pts purchased in the Public Sale and still held (staked or untransferred or unconverted) at the snapshot time.
    
*   River Pts acquired from secondary markets or transferred before the snapshot are not eligible for buyback.
    
*   Any non-eligible address sending River Pts back will be treated as invalid and not included in the buyback.
    

### [](#p-14-timeline-3)✦ Timeline

*   Nov 11 | Eligible addresses and amounts published
    
*   Nov 12 – Nov 20 | Participants send River Pts to the designated buyback address
    
*   Nov 20 – Nov 23 | Team reviews and confirms all buyback addresses and amounts
    
*   Nov 23 | River team completes all BNB buybacks and transfers to eligible addresses
