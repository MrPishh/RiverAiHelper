River has engaged in extensive review and community discussion regarding the Pts conversion mechanism.

We sincerely appreciate the constructive feedback, detailed analysis, and thoughtful proposals shared across the community.

These discussions directly shaped the upgrade directions outlined below.

The following represents the consolidated outcome of multi-party evaluation, simulation, and community input.

## [](#p-20-structural-upgrade-directions-1)**✦ Structural Upgrade Directions**

The upgraded conversion framework is being designed around four core components.

Each component responds to issues surfaced by users and aims to establish a system that is predictable, fair, and resistant to short-term pressure.

### [](#p-20-h-1-daily-conversion-cap-2)**1\. Daily Conversion Cap**

A fixed daily cap will be introduced to distribute conversion activity over time. This ensures orderly execution, prevents high-impact single-day surges, and maintains system stability.

This point was raised repeatedly by community members and is now a core parameter.

### [](#p-20-h-2-per-conversion-limit-3)**2\. Per-Conversion Limit**

Each conversion action will have a maximum allowable amount.

This prevents any single transaction from consuming a disproportionate share of the daily quota and ensures fair access across all participants.

### [](#p-20-h-3-dynamic-conversion-curve-4)**3\. Dynamic Conversion Curve**

Based on community suggestions, the static rate model will be replaced with a Dynamic Conversion Curve. Under this design, the conversion ratio adjusts according to participation density and system balance.

Higher conversion during a cycle results in proportionally adjusted rates, forming an endogenous, self-moderating mechanism shaped by collective behavior.

### [](#p-20-h-4-staked-river-activation-for-conversions-5)**4\. Staked RIVER Activation for Conversions**

When conversion resumes, all converted River Pts will go into the RIVER Staking system. This gives users governance rights and staking rewards.

Users can choose their own staking period. Staking longer will return more RIVER.

This structure keeps ownership with the user and aligns conversion outcomes with long-term incentives through governance and staking rewards.

## [](#p-20-closing-note-6)**✦ Closing Note**

Additional simulations are currently in progress, and we will share the results and detailed parameters as they are finalized.

Key elements of the upgraded mechanism will be introduced step by step to keep the process transparent and predictable.

A full activation timeline will be announced once validation is complete.
