# Important Notices and Operational Updates

---

## Precautionary Bridge Pause (April 19, 2026)

River issued a precautionary pause on the bridge as a safety measure.
**River funds and contracts were NOT affected.** The pause was preventive, not a response to any exploit or loss of funds. Normal operations resumed after the pause.

---

## ListaDAO / USDX Depeg Notice (November 6, 2025)

Following a USDX depeg event, ListaDAO initiated a standard market adjustment. River users with positions in ListaDAO's USDX-related pools were affected by this market adjustment. River's own contracts and satUSD were not compromised.

---

## All Systems Safe Notice (October 11, 2025)

During a period of market volatility, River confirmed that all systems — Omni-CDP, Smart Vault, and Prime Vault — were operating safely and stably. This was a reassurance announcement, not a response to any incident.

---

## River Pts Conversion Suspension (November 9, 2025)

Conversion was suspended on November 9, 2025 following a coordinated market manipulation attack (November 7–9). This led to the full redesign of the conversion mechanism. See the governance folder for the complete account: `governance/river-s-actions-and-follow-up-on-the-recent-market-event.md`

---

## $RIVER Pre-Launch Warning (September 20, 2025)

Before the official TGE on September 22, River issued a warning: "$RIVER is not officially live yet. Please wait for our official update. Any other so-called sources are not official." The official token did not launch until September 22, 2025.
