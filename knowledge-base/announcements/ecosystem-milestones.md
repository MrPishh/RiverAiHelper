## Brand Upgrade — Satoshi Protocol to River

River was previously known as Satoshi Protocol. This was not a rebrand — it was a brand upgrade to reflect the expanded vision of what was being built.

- **Satoshi Protocol** was the original name, focused on a Bitcoin-ecosystem stablecoin
- The original stablecoin was called **$SAT** — this became **satUSD** as the project evolved
- **$RIVER** is the governance and utility token that launched at TGE (September 22, 2025) — it was always the planned token name for the River era
- The project also cancelled its BEVM deployment and returned that investment as it moved to a broader multi-chain strategy (Ethereum, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Base, and more)

The core mission grew from a BTC-ecosystem stablecoin into the first chain-abstraction stablecoin system connecting liquidity across all major ecosystems.

## Funding History

**Seed Round — May 2024 ($2M)**
Before the brand upgrade to River, the project operated as Satoshi Protocol — a Bitcoin-ecosystem stablecoin protocol. The original stablecoin was called $SAT, which later became satUSD as the vision expanded. The seed round was led by CMS Holdings and RockTree Capital, with participation from Cypher Capital, Optic Capital, Metalpha (Bitmain parent), and angel investors Paul Taylor (ex-BlackRock) and Yenwen Feng (Perpetual Protocol). Original chain deployments at that stage included BOB, Bitlayer, and BEVM.

**Strategic Round — January 2026 ($12M)**
In January 2026, River closed a **$12M strategic round**:
- Maelstrom Fund (founded by Arthur Hayes) — announced January 5, 2026
- Justin Sun / TRON DAO — $8M investment announced January 21, 2026
- Round formally closed: January 23, 2026
