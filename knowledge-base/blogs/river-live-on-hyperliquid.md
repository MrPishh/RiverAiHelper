### What Is Hyperliquid?

Hyperliquid is a decentralized L1 blockchain built around a fully on-chain order book. It matches trades at the speed of a centralized exchange while assets stay non-custodial throughout. Beyond spot and perpetuals, the ecosystem supports borrowing, lending, RWAs, and equities via HIP-3.

-   $2.95T in cumulative volume, averaging $8.34B per day
-   1.4M+ users across perpetuals and spot markets
-   70%+ share of the on-chain perpetuals market
-   100+ assets across crypto perpetuals, spot, equities, and RWAs

$RIVER on Hyperliquid Spot means on-chain trading with no bridge dependency and no third-party custody risk.

### How to Bridge RIVER to Hyperliquid Spot ( HyperCore )

Getting RIVER onto Hyperliquid spot requires two steps, not one.

When you bridge from an external chain, RIVER lands on HyperEVM first. From there, you need to manually transfer it to HyperCore — the layer where spot trading and balance snapshots actually run.

Both steps are quick, but skipping the second one means your RIVER never reaches the spot wallet.

#### **Step 1 — Bridge RIVER to HyperEVM**

-   Navigate to Bridge [https://app.riverai.inc/bridge](https://app.riverai.inc/bridge?ref=blog.riverai.inc)
-   Bridge RIVER to HyperEVM
-   Received RIVER on HyperEVM

#### **Step 2 — Transfer from HyperEVM to HyperCore**

-   Go to Hyperliquid [https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade](https://t.co/BPGq3iJVTF?ref=blog.riverai.inc)
-   Find RIVER in your balance
-   Transfer from HyperEVM → Spot ( HyperCore )
-   Receive RIVER on Hyperliquid Spot
