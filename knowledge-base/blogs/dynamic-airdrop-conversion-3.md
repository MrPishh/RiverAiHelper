### **Dynamic Airdrop Conversion: Time as a Variable**

Most airdrops that end once tokens are claimed, River’s **Dynamic Airdrop Conversion** begins there. 

Participants received **River Pts**, an ERC-20 token they can convert to **$RIVER** anytime within 180 days. The longer they wait, the better the conversion ratio — making time itself a source of value.

Since TGE, [**River Pts have surged 40×**](https://gmgn.ai/bsc/token/0xfc6be825925b7a83d131e33b46efef9084f0e014?ref=blog.riverai.inc) **on DEX**, as the market prices in timing and strategy. Some users convert early for liquidity; others wait for higher ratios. Together, they form a **living market** that adjusts continuously, not a one-time unlock.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2025/10/data-src-image-f0ff02f5-b271-4bf5-a852-f2aa7766538d.png)

In practice, **River Pts function like a call option** on **$RIVER** — their value rises as the conversion ratio improves.

This continuous process turns the airdrop into a live market, where users can either hold River Pts for higher future conversion or **hedge $RIVER perpetuals to manage exposure**, creating a natural balance between liquidity and time-based appreciation.

### **Conversion Example — 1M River Pts**

The conversion curve shows how time relates to the amount of tokens a user receives.

Holding **1 M River Pts** from **Day 1 to Day 180** can lead to a **270× difference in $RIVER output**, depending on when conversion occurs.

This structure lets users see how time directly changes the value they receive — prices move with market behavior, not fixed unlocks.

### **Why It Matters** 

[Dynamic Airdrop Conversion](https://docs.riverai.inc/tokenomics/airdrop?ref=blog.riverai.inc)  reframes token distribution as a **market mechanism** rather than a release schedule.

It establishes a **measurable interplay between time, behavior, and liquidity**, where each user decision contributes to price formation and supply balance.

This approach moves tokenomics toward an open, self-adjusting system — one defined by participation instead of presets.
