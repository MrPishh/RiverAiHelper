River is now live on Base, bringing Omni-CDP, satUSD, and its capital coordination system to one of Ethereum’s fastest-growing Layer 2 networks.

Users can now mint satUSD using top-tier collateral, provide liquidity on Base-native DEXs, and earn River Pts at a 50x multiplier.

* * *

### **satUSD: Omni-Chain, Overcollateralized, and Native**

satUSD is an overcollateralized stablecoin backed by wETH, wBTC, clBTC (Cygnus), and cbBTC (Coinbase-wrapped BTC).

Powered by LayerZero and built on the OFT standard, satUSD supports native minting across chains. Users can deposit collateral directly on Base and mint satUSD without using bridges or wrapped assets.

After minting, satUSD can be staked, traded, or deployed across supported protocols in the Base ecosystem.

**50x River Points Incentive Now Live**

To support early liquidity, River is offering a 50x River Pts multiplier for satUSD liquidity providers on Base.

-   Add satUSD-USDT liquidity on supported Base DEXs
-   Earn swap fees and River Pts weekly
-   River Pts convert to $RIVER at TGE

This pool is among the most capital-efficient opportunities for stablecoin LPs on Base.

* * *

### **How to Get Started on Base**

1.  Visit [app.river.inc/mint](https://app.river.inc/mint?ref=blog.river.inc)
2.  Connect wallet, switch to Base
3.  Deposit wETH, wBTC, clBTC, or cbBTC
4.  Maintain a minimum 110% - 150% collateral ratio
5.  Mint satUSD and manage your position

All actions are native to Base—no bridging or wrapping required.

### **About River**

River is building a circulatory system that connects capital, yield, and contribution across chains. Through three core modules—Omni-CDP, Yield, and 4FUN—River forms a modular capital coordination layer.

It enables users to access stablecoin liquidity, earn protocol rewards, and convert participation into on-chain ownership. At the center of this system is satUSD, an overcollateralized stablecoin that powers value creation and value circulation across ecosystems.
