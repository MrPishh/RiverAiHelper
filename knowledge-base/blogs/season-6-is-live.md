River's seasonal airdrop model kept the system in motion after TGE.

Every cycle brought new participants, expanded into new ecosystems, with new adoptions through real use. That consistency came from the community, and S6 continues it.

Season 6 is now active.

* * *

### Timeline

-   S6 start date July 1 to Sep 30, 2026
-   Conversion window runs July 1 through Sep 30, 2026
-   Claim window stays open 90 days, closing Sep 30, 2026
-   S6 ideal rate will be 0.01
-   River Pts you earn during S6 become claimable after settlement
-   Final claim and distribution date confirmed through official announcement

⏳ S6 runs July 1 to September 30, the first Season to run a complete cycle under [Conversion 3.0](https://blog.riverai.inc/dynamic-airdrop-conversion-3-0/).

* * *

### How to Participate

Five Seasons built River into working infrastructure. Season 6 takes it further.

This is the Season River moves past the stablecoin, into the frontier forming now, and it carries River's most important direction yet. New products arrive here

#### 1\. New Product

River is extending its map into the next big market, the one taking shape around the technology redrawing every industry. Value there still needs rails, and people building them early.

#### 2\. RIVER Staking and Governance

Stake $RIVER to earn River Pts. Converting those River Pts into Staked RIVER gives you voting power, and that voting power earns River Pts every week.

Voting shares more River Pts on top, weighted by the voting power you hold.

#### 3\. Campaigns

Partner Campaigns run throughout S6, each with its own partner, reward structure, and multiplier.

Joining them consistently is a direct way to accumulate River Pts across the Season. Programs launch across partner ecosystems and announce through official channels.

#### 4\. Omni-CDP (satUSD)

satUSD stays the foundation. Deploy it across supported chains, mint, provide liquidity, or put it to work inside integrated DeFi protocols.

Connect your X account to River4FUN to participate. Post content, join community activities, and follow ongoing campaigns to accumulate River Pts.

It will update River Pts you earned daily, where you can track your standing through the Season.

### Dynamic Airdrop Conversion

Conversion lets you turn the River Pts you earn each Season into Staked RIVER. A few settings move as each new Season opens.

**Conversion during S6**

-   S6 runs from July 1 to Sep 30
-   Conversion window opens to 90 days
-   Conversion rate stays at 0.01
-   Staked Until dates step forward one quarter, from January 1, 2027 out to October 1, 2028
