At River, our mission is to **connect every asset to its opportunity**.

With our chain-abstraction stablecoin system, **satUSD** has already become a shared currency across ecosystems, backed by over **$700M in assets**.

As more institutions explore on-chain yield, they ask the same question:  
How can we participate with full security and compliance?

This is why we built **Prime Vault**.

### What is Prime Vault

Prime Vault works in a way that feels familiar to Smart Vault users:

-   Deposit assets
-   The system auto-converts into satUSD and channels it into yield
-   Users receive periodic returns

The difference is in security standards designed for institutions:

-   **Custodian Protection**: Assets remain with leading custodians under regulation and insurance coverage
-   **Zero Liquidation Risk**: Automated management ensures stability even in volatile markets
-   **Transparent Yield**: Generated from satUSD staking pools and yield strategy, not unsustainable subsidies.

Today, more than **$250M in assets have already been deposited into Prime Vault**, underscoring the strong demand from institutional participants.

### **How Does Prime Vault Work?**

Prime Vault connects **institutional-grade security** with **sustainable yield access**.

Institutions can deposit assets into the system, automatically mint & stake satUSD, access yield opportunities with custodian wallet.

This enables institutions to earn passive returns while their capital stays protected by top-tier custodians and a zero-liquidation mechanism.
