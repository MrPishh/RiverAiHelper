River has integrated LayerZero to enable native, cross-chain minting of satUSD through its core module: **Omni-CDP**.

Stablecoin users often face a fragmented experience. Assets sit idle on one chain while opportunities exist on another. Bridging adds risk. Wrapping adds complexity.

**River solves this.**

Users can deposit BTC, ETH, BNB, or LSTs on one chain and mint satUSD directly on another. No bridges. No wrappers. Just native, secure access to stablecoin liquidity across multiple ecosystems.

* * *

### **What You Can Do with River**

-   Deposit assets on any supported chain
-   Mint satUSD on the chain where you need liquidity
-   Transfer satUSD across chains without third-party bridges (no wrap, no risk, no slippage)
-   Use satUSD in DeFi for trading, staking, lending, and 30+ DeFi protocols
-   Move between ecosystems with unified pricing and no slippage risk

### **How LayerZero Powers Omnichain Liquidity**

LayerZero is an interop protocol designed to enable efficient, lightweight and secure communication between blockchains. It provides the foundation for River's omnichain liquidity management, ensuring secure movement of assets across multiple ecosystems.

River deployed OApp through LayerZero, using \_lzSend and \_lzReceive functions to manage cross-chain collateral, track debt positions in real time, and maintain stability in diverse market conditions.

satUSD implements the OFT (Omnichain Fungible Token) standard, ensuring uniform value across different blockchains.

satUSD is built on the OFT (Omnichain Fungible Token) standard, ensuring consistent value across all supported chains. This removes the need for wrapped assets or third-party bridges, eliminating slippage and custodial risk.

With LayerZero, River connects fragmented assets across chains and enables satUSD to move securely and natively across DeFi.
