River, the circulatory system for crypto, is now live on Sonic—an ultra-fast Layer 1 built by the Fantom team.

This launch expands satUSD access and brings River’s stablecoin, yield, and contribution infrastructure into Sonic’s high-throughput, low-fee ecosystem.

**What’s New on Sonic**

-   **Mint satUSD** natively using wBTC or wETH as collateral
-   Swap USDC / USDT for satUSD at 1:1 ratio
-   **Provide satUSD-USDC** liquidity and earn S2 Airdrop / Incentive from Shadow
-   **Earn River Pts**, convertible to $RIVER at TGE

Built on LayerZero’s OFT standard, satUSD enables seamless, omni-chain minting without bridges or wrappers.

-   satUSD Contract ( Sonic ) : `0xb4818BB69478730EF4e33Cc068dD94278e2766cB`

* * *

### **Why Sonic**

Sonic delivers sub-second finality, 2,000+ TPS, and near-zero fees. Its EVM compatibility and developer-aligned incentives make it a powerful environment for DeFi growth.

River’s deployment brings stablecoin-based capital coordination to Sonic’s performance-driven infrastructure.
