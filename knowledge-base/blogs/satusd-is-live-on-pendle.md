### What is satUSD & satUSD+

satUSD is River’s over-collateralized stablecoin, backed by BTC, ETH, and BNB.

satUSD+ is the yield-bearing version — it accrues stable yield daily, paid in USDT.

Starting now, satUSD+ is live on **Pendle**.

You can trade its future yield, lock in a fixed APY, or farm River Points — all in one market.

* * *

### **Why Pendle?**

Pendle is DeFi’s yield marketplace. It splits any yield-bearing token into two:

-   **PT (Principal Token):** Buy at a discount, redeem 1:1 at maturity. You lock in a fixed return.
-   **YT (Yield Token):** Earns the yield stream. The more yield satUSD+ generates, the more YT pays.

With satUSD+ now live on Pendle (BNB Chain), users can choose between fixed-income strategies or higher-upside yield farming.

### How River Pts Work

River Pts are weekly rewards distributed to satUSD users based on actual on-chain actions, not just passive holding. Every Saturday, 2,000,000 River Pts are allocated proportionally according to user behavior scores.

Each action has a different multiplier:

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2025/06/CleanShot-2025-06-19-at-00.17.01.jpg)

River Pts multiplier overview

The more scores you earn relative to others, the more River Pts you receive.
