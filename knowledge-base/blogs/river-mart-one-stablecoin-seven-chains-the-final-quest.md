### What Is River?

**River** is building the first **chain-abstraction stablecoin system** — designed to solve the liquidity fragmentation of multi-chain DeFi.

-   Users can deposit BTC, ETH, BNB, or LSTs on **Chain A**
-   Then mint the stablecoin **satUSD** natively on **Chain B**
-   No bridging. No wrapping. Just clean, native access.

It’s powered by **Omni-CDP**, integrated with **LayerZero**, and designed to support real liquidity, yield, and utility across ecosystems.

River isn’t a single-chain app. It’s a chain-abstraction stablecoin system.
