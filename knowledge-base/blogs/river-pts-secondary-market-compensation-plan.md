River is publishing the Secondary Market Compensation Plan to address losses incurred by users who actively traded River Pts on the secondary market.

This plan is based strictly on **verifiable on-chain data** and **submitted user records**. It is designed to compensate **real trading losses**.

Users who completed the official compensation form by February 28, 2026 and met the eligibility criteria outlined below are included in this compensation process.

### Compensation Plan Overview

-   229 submissions received
-   Total pool: **20,000 Staked RIVER**
-   Compensation is based on **net trading cost** from River Pts secondary market activity
-   Distribution is proportional and delivered as Staked RIVER

### **Eligibility**

Two conditions are required:

1.  The address submitted the official compensation form by February 28, 2026
2.  The address has verifiable River Pts secondary market trading activity during the calculation period

Addresses that meet both conditions are included in the compensation distribution.

### **Distribution Schedule**

All compensation is distributed as Staked RIVER in a single distribution.

From the day of distribution, your RIVER begins earning staking rewards and carries governance weight immediately.

Your total compensation is claimable in four parts

Compensation Claim Overview

Throughout this period, your Staked RIVER continues to earn River Pts rewards weekly and contributes to your governance participation. Once the allocation is finalized, distribution is automatic. 

### **How the Calculation Works**

All River Pts secondary market transactions within a fixed time window were reviewed.

**Calculation period**: September 22 – November 9, 6:00 PM UTC+8

Only transactions within this window are counted.

For each eligible address, two values were calculated:

-   Total cost spent buying River Pts on the secondary market
-   Total value received from selling River Pts on the secondary market

The difference is the **net trading cost** for that address. Your net trading cost determines your share of the compensation pool.

Addresses with higher net trading costs receive a larger share of the 20,000 Staked RIVER pool.

### **Example**

If the total net trading cost across all eligible addresses is 1,000,000 units and one address accounts for 10,000 units, that address represents 1% of the total.

It receives 1% of the pool — **200 RIVER**, distributed as Staked RIVER across the quarterly schedule.

### **FAQ**

#### **Q: How do I know if I will receive compensation?**

Addresses that submitted the official form by the deadline and have verifiable secondary market trading activity during the calculation period will automatically receive Staked RIVER.

#### **Q: Why is my allocation smaller than expected?**

Allocations are proportional. Each address's share depends on its net trading cost relative to all other eligible addresses.

#### **Q: Is this an airdrop?**

This is a compensation plan specifically designed to address secondary market trading losses, distributed based on verified on-chain trading data.

#### **Q: What do I earn while my RIVER is staked?**

Staked RIVER earns River Pts rewards weekly and carries governance weight. You are actively participating in River governance and earning rewards from the day your compensation is distributed.

#### **Q: Do I need to take any action to receive my RIVER?**

Once the allocation is finalized, Staked RIVER is distributed automatically to eligible addresses. Staking rewards begin accumulating immediately.
