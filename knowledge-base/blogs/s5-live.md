### **How to Participate**

S5 centers on $RIVER staking.

Most of the incentive weight in this Season sits with partner programs and campaigns, which means active participation in Campaigns is the most efficient path to earning River Pts.

### 1\. $RIVER Staking + Governance

The core track in S5. Staking $RIVER is how you accumulate River Pts, gain voting power, and participate in River governance.

Longer staking commitments retain more of your base output and carry stronger voting power, so the staking duration you choose directly shapes both your River Pts accumulation and how much voice you carry.

Voting power scales with both staked RIVER and commitment duration.

100 RIVER staked for 12 months carries 800 voting power. Add more or extend your commitment at any time, and the weekly snapshot reflects it in the next governance cycle.

-   Stake $RIVER to earn rewards and River Pts
-   Governance votes weighted by staked RIVER and commitment duration
-   Staking outputs remain claimable for 3 months

### 2\. Partner Campaigns

Where most new incentives land in S5.

Partner Campaigns drive most of the new River Pts this Season. Each one runs with its own partner and multiplier, and joining them consistently is the fastest way to accumulate Pts in S5. 

-   Programs launch throughout the Season across partner ecosystems
-   Each program carries its own reward structure and multipliers
-   Announcements publish through official channels

### **3\. Omni-CDP (satUSD)**

satUSD stays the foundation. Deploy it across supported chains, mint, provide liquidity, or use it inside integrated DeFi protocols.

Weekly snapshots run every Saturday. The S5 Leaderboard updates after each snapshot.

S5 also brings satUSD into new ecosystems and protocol integrations. Each expansion adds more venues for deployment, more ways to participate, and more surface for River Pts across the Season.
