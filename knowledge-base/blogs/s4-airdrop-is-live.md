### What is River Pts

River Pts is the ecosystem points of River, issued in ERC-20 format. River Pts accrue based on your contributions, DeFi activity, joining River4FUN social campaigns, contributing through partner programs, and providing liquidity across supported chains.

At each seasonal airdrop, accumulated Pts convert to $RIVER. Full Conversion 3.0 details release once the mechanism is ready.

River Pts contract address : `0xfc6be825925b7a83d131e33b46efef9084f0e014`

### Dynamic Airdrop Conversion 3.0

Dynamic Airdrop Conversion 3.0 is the first seasonal incentives model from River.

It changes how airdrops work, from a single distribution to a structure that runs every season. In a classic airdrop, the cycle ends after one claim.

Conversion 3.0 works differently. Each season, River Pts accrue through ecosystem activity and you can decide when to convert it to $RIVER. Contributors who stay active across seasons keep adding to what they earn, season after season.

[Season 5 is live](https://blog.riverai.inc/s5-live/) and River Pts are already accruing.

#### Claim Window

The window stays open for 60 days from May 1. After June 30, the S4 allocation closes permanently.

Unclaimed River Pts at the end of the window are treated as forfeited.

The S4 allocation cannot be reopened, recovered, or carried over to S5 under any circumstance. Make sure to claim before the window closes.

### Conclusion

S4 closes a four-month cycle and contributors will be able to get RIVER via Conversion 3.0. Claim your River Pts earned during S4 within 60 days window.

Season 5 is already accruing for the contributors who stay.
