### How to Earn River Pts in S3

From **post-TGE (2025-09-22 onward)**, all newly earned River Pts count toward S3.

⏳ **Claim timeline**: expected in about 90 days, but the actual date may be earlier or later. River will announce the final timing.

#### 1\. Omni-CDP

The core is **using satUSD**:

-   Add liquidity to pools like satUSD/USDC or satUSD/WBTC;
-   Stake into **Smart Vault** or **Prime Vault**;
-   Hold YT (Yield Token).

Each action comes with a **2x–50x multiplier** depending on type.

River Pts are tracked through weekly snapshots, with updates typically published over the weekend.

We will also organize special campaigns such as a [10M RIVER trading competition](https://x.com/RiverdotInc/status/1970117812460118385?ref=blog.river.inc) where participants can earn additional River Pts. Please follow River’s official announcements for the latest details.
