### **How to Participate in the Season 4 Airdrop**

Season 4 consolidates incentives around three core pillars, with a clear priority on active capital deployment and staking alignment.

#### **1\. Omni-CDP ( satUSD )** 

satUSD remains the foundation of River’s system and the primary yield-generating asset.

Users can participate through multiple on-chain actions, including:

-   Providing liquidity in pools such as satUSD / USDT / USDC
-   Use satUSD across integrated DeFi protocols 
-   Mint or stake satUSD on River

Reward multipliers range from **2× to 25×**, depending on strategy and pool configuration. Periodic campaigns, such as trading competitions, may be introduced.

All activity is tracked via **weekly snapshots** and reflected on the [**Leaderboard**](https://app.riverai.inc/leaderboard?ref=blog.riverai.inc).

#### **2\. $RIVER Staking**

In Season 4, staking $RIVER will be the core participation method for long-term alignment.

Users participate by:

-   Claiming rewards on a regular basis
-   Earning $RIVER staking rewards
-   Staking $RIVER

Staking rewards must be **claimed within 3 months**. Any rewards that remain unclaimed after this period will expire.

This mechanism ensures staking incentives are directed toward users who remain active and aligned with the protocol over time.

#### **Closing**

Season 4 is structured around clear participation paths and predictable mechanics.

-   **satUSD Earn** continues to reward active satUSD usage through liquidity, minting, and supported strategies, tracked via weekly snapshots.
-   **$RIVER staking** serves as the primary alignment mechanism, with rewards distributed on a recurring basis and subject to a three-month claim window.
-   **River4FUN** remains active, allowing users to earn rewards through content and community participation, with details announced per campaign.

Season 4 begins on **December 22**, following the Season 3 snapshot on December 19, and is expected to run for 90–120 days. Users who remain active across this period will be positioned to receive the full range of Season 4 incentives.

Further details, including campaign updates and final claim timing, will be updated through official River channels.

Powered by the omni-CDP stablecoin satUSD, River allows users to collateralize assets on one chain and mint on another — natively earning, leveraging, and scaling across networks.
