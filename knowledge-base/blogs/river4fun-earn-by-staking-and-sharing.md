River4FUN is the contribution layer of River, designed to reward users not just for capital, but for participation and influence.

By staking assets and engaging on X (Twitter), users earn River Pts, that convert into the governance token $RIVER at TGE.

This guide explains how River4FUN works and how you can start earning today.

* * *

### How River4FUN Works

River4FUN operates through two coordinated systems: a staking pool and a social engagement pool.

Each tracks different types of contribution, and both reward users with River Pts.

Connect your X account to start.

The system assigns you a tier based on past engagement. From there, daily activity is scored using key signals:

-   Post frequency
-   Impressions and reach
-   Replies and interactions
-   Content quality

These inputs generate your **Mindshare Score**, which determines your share of the reward pool each day.

### Staking Pool

Users can deposit supported assets, such as wBNB, USD1 and others, into the staking pool with no lock-up. Funds remain liquid and withdrawable at any time.

River Pts are distributed continuously on a per-block basis. The earlier you join, the fewer participants you compete with, increasing your reward share.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2025/05/CleanShot-2025-05-30-at-19.57.08@2x.jpg)

Stake River Pts

Each day, the protocol adjusts River Pts emissions based on project's mindshare

**Claim and stake**: Instantly reinvested in the River Pts Vault for higher APR, locks until TGE. These convert directly into $RIVER.
