### Extending Dynamic Airdrop Conversion

The River Pts Public Sale is the continuation of Dynamic Airdrop Conversion — a system that turned time into a measurable variable of value.

If Dynamic Airdrop Conversion redefined how distribution works, the Public Sale redefines how price is discovered. It expands the curve beyond time and behavior, allowing the market itself to determine value through open participation.

Every bid, every participant, every moment becomes part of the same value curve — a continuous process where future $RIVER is no longer assigned by design, but emerges through the market’s collective action.

### **Public Sale Overview** — Dutch Auction

The Public Sale introduces a **Dutch Auction**, running for **48 hours**.

-   **Start Time:** Oct 29, 14:00 (UTC)
-   **Auction Type:** 48-hour Dutch Auction on **BNB Chain**
-   **Purchase Option:** BNB
-   **Asset:** River Pts

Price decreases gradually over time. Participants can join at any moment before the sale ends by selecting the amount of River Pts they wish to purchase and paying with **BNB**.

-   All participants settles at the **the final (lowest ) auction price**.
-   All River Pts and refunds claimable right after the 48-hour auction ends

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2025/10/image-30.png)

River Pts Public Sale details

**Example (for illustration only):**

If a participant buys 100,000 River Pts at $1 and the final auction price ends at $0.5,

the participant receives **100,000 River Pts** and a **refund of $50,000 BNB** — the difference between the bid and the final auction price.

Participants claim their River Pts allocation and refund for any price difference after the auction ends.

_(Note: All prices above are for demonstrating the Dutch Auction model only; the actual starting and reserve prices will be announced prior to launch.)_

The Public Sale includes a **tier-based referral system** linked to River4FUN Season 2 rankings. Your X username is your referral code.

-   **Referral rewards:** 2–12% in bonus River Pts
-   **Tier-based:** Higher tiers (e.g., _Legend_) earn higher rewards
-   **Example:**
