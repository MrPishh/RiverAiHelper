### How It Works

Three voting rounds. Stake RIVER to vote for the teams you think will advance and the champion you back.

Each round closes before the matches that decide it. Vote for your team before the deadline, and the final results post after each round.

| You Vote For | Voting Window | Result | Reward Pool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 32 teams that reach the knockout stage | Jun 5 - Jun 11 | Jun 27 | 150K River Pts |
| 8 teams that reach the quarterfinals | Jun 27 - Jun 30 | Jul 11 | 150K River Pts |
| The champion | Jul 11 - Jul 14 | Jul 19 | 150K River Pts |

### Rewards

Each round pool is 150K River Pts. The three rounds run independently for a total of 450K River Pts.

**Voting power = staked RIVER × multiplier**

The multiplier rises with the staking epoch, 1x (3 months) - 24x (24 months)

-   Place your voting power on one team or spread it across several teams
-   When a team you vote for reaches the round target, your voting power on it shares the round pool
-   River Pts rewards add to S5 leaderboard, credited to your voting address

* * *

### Examples

Voting power comes from how much RIVER you stake and how long you stake it. Stake 100 RIVER for 24 months and the 24x multiplier gives you 2,400 voting power.

Say the round pool is 150,000 River Pts, and the total voting power on every team that advances adds up to 100,000. Here is how the same 2,400 plays out two ways.

| You cast 2,400 as | Outcome | Votes that count | Your share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All on one team | Team advances | 2,400 | 3,600 River Pts |
| 800 on each of three teams | Two advance, one out | 1,600 | 2,400 River Pts |

Your share is the pool times your winning votes over that total, so 150,000 × (2,400 / 100,000) = 3,600 and 150,000 × (1,600 / 100,000) = 2,400

Only votes on teams that advance count, so in the spread case the 800 on the team that goes out earns nothing.
