### How Voting Works

The campaign runs across **two weekly rounds**, from April 27 to May 11.

-   Voting is open to any address holding Staked RIVER
-   Each address votes once per round
-   Two rounds total, snapshots refresh weekly

Voting power is derived directly from the staking position on-chain. The formula follows the [River Staking Parameters for Activation](https://gov.riverai.inc/t/river-staking-parameters-for-activation/147?ref=blog.riverai.inc)

**Voting power = staked amount × multiplier**

| Epoch | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 3 Months | 1x |
| 6 Months | 2x |
| 9 Months | 4x |
| 12 Months | 8x |

Longer commitment carries heavier weight. The same logic that governs River Staking applies here.

#### Weekly Snapshots

Each round uses the previous Saturday's snapshot.

-   Round 1 (April 27 to May 4) uses the **April 25** snapshot
-   Round 2 (May 4 to May 11) uses the **May 2** snapshot

Because snapshots refresh weekly, participants can adjust between rounds. Extending an epoch, increasing stake, or both, all reflect in the following snapshot.

#### Worked Example

An address stakes **1,000 RIVER for 6 months** before voting opens.

| Round | Snapshot | Stake | Multiplier | Voting Power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Round 1 | April 25 | 1,000 RIVER | 2x | 2,000 |
| Round 2 | May 2 | 1,000 RIVER | 2x | 2,000 |

Same address opens a second position of **500 RIVER for 12 months** before May 2:

| Round | Snapshot | Positions | Voting Power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Round 1 | April 25 | 1,000 RIVER, 6 months (2x) | 2,000 |
| Round 2 | May 2 | 1,000 RIVER, 6 months (2x)  
\+ 500 RIVER, 12 months (8x) | 6,000 |
| Total across both rounds | 8,000 |

Total voting power across both rounds: **8,000**, compared to 4,000 without the additional stake.

The design rewards continued commitment throughout the voting period, not just position at a single point in time.

### Reward Distribution

The Top 30 receive a pyramid distribution across four tiers, weighted by final vote share. Top 10 carry approximately **77% of both pools**.

Rewards will add to each winner's S5 balance on River4fun, linked through their connected X handle.

| Rank | Per Creator | Tier RIVER | Tier River Pts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #1 | $300 RIVER + 40,000 River Pts | $300 | 40,000 |
| #2 to #3 | $175 RIVER + 20,000 River Pts | $350 | 40,000 |
| #4 to #10 | $75 RIVER + 5,000 River Pts | $525 | 35,000 |
| #11 to #30 | ~$16.25 RIVER + 1,750 River Pts | $325 | 35,000 |
| Total |  | $1,500 | 150,000 |

Final ranking is determined by aggregated votes across both rounds. Rewards are distributed within one week of voting closing.
