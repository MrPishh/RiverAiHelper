# Blog: satoshi-protocol-raises-2m-in-seed-round-funding

Satoshi Protocol, the universal stablecoin built for the Bitcoin ecosystem, has successfully completed a $2 million seed round funding in May 2024.

The round was led by CMS Holdings and RockTree Capital, with participation from Cypher Capital, Optic Capital, Side Door Ventures, Metalpha (NASDAQ: MATH, parent company: Bitmain), Outliers Fund, Comma3 ventures, and angel investors including Paul Taylor (ex-BlackRock) and Yenwen Feng (Perpetual Protocol).

### **What is Satoshi Protocol**

Satoshi Protocol is a revolutionary "universal" stablecoin protocol backed by Bitcoin. It allows users to deposit BTC and LST as collateral to mint the stablecoin $SAT on both Bitcoin mainnet and multiple L2s.

### **Key Developments and Major Updates**

#### **First Utility and Stable Asset on Bitcoin Mainnet**

Powered by Runes, Satoshi Protocol is pioneering the first utility and stable asset on the Bitcoin mainnet.

#### **Extensive Integrations with Leading BTC Ecosystem**

-   Mainnet: Integrated with BOB, Bitlayer and BEVM.
-   Testnet: Collaborating with Core Chain, Botanix, B^2, Anduro (incubated by MARA), and Omni Network.

#### **Binance Web3 Wallet Campaign**

First BTC layer2 stablecoin protocol to collaborate with Binance Web3 Wallet Campaign, with over 172K users completing tasks with BEVM.

#### **Robust User Base**

-   100K+ Twitter followers.
-   200K+ total users since March.
-   22K+ active addresses and 5K+ DAU on mainnet since April.

#### **Collaboration with Bybit Web3 Wallets**

First Bitcoin L2 stablecoin protocol to collaborate with Bybit Web3 Wallets.

#### **Novel Architecture with Omni Network**

Partnered with Omni Network to create an innovative architecture that bridges Ethereum liquidity to BTC stable and vice versa, enhancing platform versatility and liquidity.

#### **Security and Integration Milestones**

Phase 1 Integration and Security check: Successfully completed the risk management and auditing with multiple defense lines with multiple in June Q2.

Phase 2 (current stage): Focusing on attracting more liquidity from liquidity providers across multiple L2s/L1 in July Q3.

Phase 3: Nexus Yield Module (NYM) for yield revenue with USDC/USDT as collateral to swap with $SAT 1:1 development and announcement at the end of Q3.

Phase 4: Circulating across whole Bitcoin Mainnet and execution layers with L2s to adopt NYM in Q4.

#### **New Partnership with Alys/Anduro (Marathon Holdings)**

Excited to announce this partnership, along with sponsoring Bitcoin2024 for increased exposure and credibility.
