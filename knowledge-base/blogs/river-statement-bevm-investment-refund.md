In 2024, River (previously Satoshi Protocol) was invited to deploy on BEVM and received ecosystem support from its foundation. As strategies evolved, both River and BEVM agreed to cancel the cooperation, and the corresponding investment was returned.

Today, River has advanced into a chain-abstraction stablecoin system deployed across Ethereum, BNB, Arbitrum, Base, Hemi and BOB.

Our current investors, including CMS, Waterdrip, Cypher, Outliers and Cogitent, have directly invested in River. River operates as an independent team with backing from these investors.

* * *

2024 年，River（當時為 Satoshi Protocol）曾受邀在 BEVM 部署，並接受基金會生態支持。隨後因 BEVM 方向調整，該合作已**完全結束，並已全額退還投資款項**。

目前，River 已升級為鏈抽象穩定幣系統，落地於 Ethereum、BNB、Arbitrum、Base、BOB 等多個生態。現有投資人包括 CMS、Waterdrip、Cypher、Outliers、Comma3 等，均直接投資 River **團隊**。
