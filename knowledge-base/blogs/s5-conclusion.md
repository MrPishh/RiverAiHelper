# Blog: s5-conclusion
Source: https://blog.riverai.inc/s5-conclusion/
Scraped: 2026-09-04T10:50:25.556Z

---

S5 opened on April 22, 2026, the day after the S4 snapshot closed, and ran through June 30, 2026. Staking and governance ran live across the season. By June 2, ~$10M worth of RIVER was staked and voting in governance.

S5 also moved satUSD into real stores.

Two payment integrations went live, and satUSD reached checkout across Argentina, Vietnam, Brazil, and the Philippines, where users bought [pizza](https://x.com/RiverdotInc/status/2060690870702379363?s=20&ref=blog.riverai.inc), [coffee](https://x.com/RiverdotInc/status/2063897453527777725?s=20&ref=blog.riverai.inc), and [World Cup jerseys](https://x.com/RiverdotInc/status/2066831881862320455?s=20&ref=blog.riverai.inc) in one tap, the way they spend cash.

S5 is the transition season into [Conversion 3.0](https://blog.riverai.inc/dynamic-airdrop-conversion-3-0/), River's first seasonal incentive model. Conversion 3.0 balances contributions and sustains token distribution across recurring Seasons.

This recap walks through the products, milestones, and ecosystem growth from the season.

* * *

### S5 Participation Overview

Eligible participation shifted further toward staking and governance compared to S4. The on-chain user base was more active across the season.

-   82k users participated in S5
-   ~1.6M RIVER staked (~$10M worth)
-   ~40M River Pts converted during S5

### Token and Staking Milestones

S5 is the first season to run on [Conversion 3.0](https://blog.riverai.inc/dynamic-airdrop-conversion-3-0/). The model balances rewards and growth across recurring Seasons, and sustains the tokenomics and expansion over the long term.

Stake size grew steadily through May and June, holding around $10M worth of RIVER by late June.

### Conclusion

S5 moved River from onchain mechanics to real-world use. Staked $RIVER reached ~$10M and moved into active governance, launch a recurring seasonal distribution, and River showed up for the community at 10 meetups from Tokyo to Buenos Aires.

The real milestone was spending. For the first time, satUSD became money people use, paid at merchants across Argentina, Vietnam, Brazil, and the Philippines without anyone selling their BTC or ETH.

A stablecoin that holds its value and still gets spent in daily life is the point River has been building toward, and S5 is where it landed.

S6 opens the next chapter. River will expand the product map, with AI among the directions ahead and a focus that points where the next season is headed.

To everyone who staked, voted, and came out this season, thank you for being part of the journey. See you in [Season 6](https://blog.riverai.inc/season-6-is-live/).
