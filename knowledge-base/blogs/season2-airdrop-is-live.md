## How to Earn River Pts

River Pts are distributed across three main tracks, each with 30% allocation, plus 10% for campaigns and partners.

### ① Use Omni-CDP (30% of total)

This track rewards users who actively mint, stake, lend, or provide liquidity with satUSD. These actions create real demand and depth for the stablecoin, and directly support River’s capital layer.

We want to reward users who move capital, not just hold it.

**Example**: Mint 200 satUSD with ETH or BTC, stake it on River, and provide satUSD-USDT liquidity on PancakeSwap. All of these count toward your River Pts.

### ② Stake in River4FUN Pools (30%)

River4FUN allows any trending token on BNB Chain to be staked for River Pts.

We call this the staking pool. If you’re part of a new project or community, this is the easiest way to earn with high APR. Rewards are shared between token holders and builders.

**Example**: Stake 1,000 Mubarak or 500 TST on River4FUN. You’ll start earning Pts daily, based on how much and how long you stake.

### ③ Connect and Post with River4FUN (30%)

This is the engagement pool. Connect your X account on River4FUN, and post about River or any partner project you like.

We’ll reward accounts that contribute consistently — not just by impressions, but by quality and creativity. Posting = earning.

### ④ Campaigns and Ecosystem Missions (10%)

The remaining 10% is set aside for campaigns, quests, and collabs.

Expect giveaway, competitions, and exclusive missions with partners across the Bitcoin, BNB, EVM ecosystems.
