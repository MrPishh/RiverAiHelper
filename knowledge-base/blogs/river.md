## What is River?

**River is the circulatory system for your crypto.**

It connects capital, delivers yield, and turns contributions into rewards — the system for the full cycle of on-chain participation.

With River, you can:

-   **Deposit** BTC, ETH, BNB, or LSTs as collateral — on the chain you already use
-   **Mint** satUSD natively on your destination chain — no bridges, no wrapping
-   **Stake** satUSD to earn yield from protocol strategies
-   **Post and provide liquidity** to earn $RIVER and shape the system

River replaces fragmented workflows with one system.

It simplifies capital flow, aligns incentives, and rewards every action that grows the network.

## **What We’ve Built**

### **Omni-CDP**

Omni-CDP is the core of River’s system. It lets users deposit BTC, ETH, BNB, or LSTs on one chain and mint satUSD on another.

The process is native, fast, and does not rely on wrapped assets or third-party bridges.

River uses LayerZero messaging and the OFT standard to handle collateral syncing and satUSD issuance across supported chains.

This structure removes the need for bridges and allows users to interact directly with the chain where they need liquidity.

> — River (@RiverdotInc) [February 11, 2025](https://twitter.com/RiverdotInc/status/1889398437634072818?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw&ref=blog.riverai.inc)

Compared to systems like Ethena, Maker, and Usual, River offers three distinct advantages:

-   **Cross-chain minting :** Users deposit on Chain A and mint on Chain B without wrapping or bridging.
-   **Multi-asset collateral :** Supports BTC, ETH, BNB, and major LSTs. This expands the range of capital that can be deployed into stablecoin issuance.
-   **Integrated yield design :** satUSD staking yield comes from protocol revenues. This gives users access to structured returns beyond stability.

In addition to minting, users can also **swap USDT or USDC for satUSD at 1:1** on supported chains, making satUSD easily accessible without using collateral.

For example, a user can deposit wBTC on Ethereum, mint satUSD on BNB Chain, or simply swap USDC into satUSD — and immediately use that liquidity in DeFi or stake it for yield.

No waiting, no asset transfer delays.

satUSD isn’t just a stablecoin. It’s a native onchain instrument for liquidity, yield, and participation.

### **satUSD Staking**

**satUSD isn’t just for liquidity — it’s built to earn.**

Users can stake satUSD to receive **satUSD+**, a yield-bearing version of the stablecoin.

Yield is sourced from protocol revenue, including minting fees, liquidation penalties, redemption fees, and other system-level income.

This makes satUSD a productive on-chain asset — held not just for stability, but for return.

It’s built for users, LPs, and DAOs who want to stay liquid while earning real yield.

### **4FUN**

4FUN is River’s contribution layer. It rewards influence, not just capital.

By linking your X account and posting about River or other projects you support, you earn River Points based on impressions, engagement, and consistency. No deposits or transactions are required.

Points convert into $RIVER through seasonal checkpoints. 4FUN turns social impact into ownership and invites anyone to contribute to River’s growth through visibility rather than liquidity.
