River's fixed-yield stablecoin asset, **PT-satUSD+** is now accepted as collateral in a new ListaDAO lending market paired with USDT, curated by **MEV Capital**.

This market allows users to **borrow USDT by supplying PT-satUSD+**, enabling yield holders to unlock liquidity without sacrificing fixed returns.

### What is satUSD+ PT?

**PT-satUSD+** is the **Principal Token** you receive when depositing satUSD+ into Pendle. It locks in the fixed yield of satUSD+ and is redeemable 1:1 at maturity (e.g. 11SEP2025).

### Why This Market Matters

In a bull cycle, users want exposure to BTC, ETH, and BNB upside—**without selling**—while still putting capital to work.

This lending market unlocks a full, yield-bearing pipeline for satUSD users:

1.  **Deposit BTC/BNB** into River → mint satUSD
2.  **Stake satUSD** → receive satUSD+ (yield-bearing stablecoin)
3.  **Split satUSD+** on Pendle / Swap satUSD+ for PT-satUSD+
4.  **Supply PT-satUSD+ to Lista** → borrow USDT against your fixed-yield position
5.  **Use USDT** for farming, trading, looping—while satUSD+ yield remains locked in via PT

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2025/07/image-1.png)

Stake → PT → Borrow

This model enables DeFi users to:

-   Preserve long exposure to volatile assets
-   Monetize stablecoin yield via PT
-   Access on-demand liquidity via USDT
-   Stack strategies without breaking the yield path

It’s a capital-efficient primitive for builders, LPs, and treasury managers who want to **extract fixed income from satUSD+ while staying liquid**.
