2026 begins after a strong year for River.

In 2025, River launched its first Omni-CDP, grew TVL to over **$600M**, and completed the $RIVER token launch, with more than **100,000 users** eligible for the airdrop.

With the core system live, we’re starting the year by inviting the community to look forward.

To kick off 2026, we’re running a New Year challenge that rewards market insight and real creativity, with a total rewards pool of $2026 RIVER + $2026 USDT.

### Campaign Details

This campaign has two rewards pool. You share how you see River in 2026, and you submit a price prediction for $RIVER.

-   **Duration:** January 2 – January 12
-   **Price Settlement:** January 15, 12:00 PM (UTC+0), using CoinMarketCap as the reference.
-   **Winners Announcement:** January 16

### Rewards Pool

We’ve split rewards into two pools.

#### **Pool A | Price Prediction ($2,026 RIVER)**

A total of $**2,026 RIVER** is allocated to this pool. We will select up to 20 predictions closest to the settlement price. Each winner receives ~$**101 RIVER**.

-   **Winners:** Up to 20 closest predictors.
-   **Reward:** Each winner will receive approximately $**101 RIVER**.

#### **Pool B | River Visions (2,026 USDT)**

A total of **2,026 USDT** is allocated to this pool. We will reward **100 submissions** that stand out for originality and creativity. Each winner receives **20.26 USDT**.

-   **Winners:** 200 creative and impactful visionaries.
-   **Reward:** **20.26 USDT** each.

* * *

### How to Participate

Follow these steps to lock in your entry:

Tweet your 2026 vision for River. We encourage all forms of creativity—whether it’s a short story, a custom graphic, a video, or even a meme. Be sure to tag `@RiverdotInc` and `@River4FUN`

#### **Step 2. Lock in your price prediction**

Decide on the **$RIVER price** you’re predicting for **January 15, 12:00 PM (UTC+0)**. Make sure the price in your tweet matches what you submit in the form.

If multiple entries land on the same price, **earlier submissions will be prioritized** when selecting winners.

**Step 3. Fill the Form**

Fill out the form with your tweet link, your price prediction, and the wallet address where you want rewards sent.

Google form → [https://forms.gle/MJ2JWUEdUqj3JeNF9](https://forms.gle/MJ2JWUEdUqj3JeNF9?ref=blog.riverai.inc)

### Rules and disclaimer

-   The final settlement price is the CoinMarketCap price at **January 15, 12:00 PM (UTC+0)**. The team will keep a timestamped snapshot or recording as the final reference.
-   If multiple participants submit the same predicted price, only the first five submissions for that exact price are eligible for Pool A. Pool A will have a maximum of 20 winners.
-   Pool B winners are selected by Team. AI-generated spam, bot-driven engagement, duplicate content, or low-effort submissions will not be considered.
-   Only one entry is allowed per person. Any attempt to use multiple accounts or Sybil tactics will result in disqualification from both pools. River reserves the right of final interpretation for this event.

### Closing

River is building infrastructure that lets liquidity move across chains without friction. This challenge is a simple way to start the year with the people who are thinking and building alongside us.

Share how you see 2026, submit your prediction, and we’ll see who read the flow right.
