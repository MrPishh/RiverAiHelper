### **The Smart Vault: One-Click Yield, Zero Liquidation**

Smart Vault was built around a single idea:

Yield should be simple — no debt positions, no liquidation risk, no manual strategies.

Here’s how it works:

1.  **Deposit assets** — this round: USDT on Ethereum
2.  **Protocol mints satUSD internally**, pegged to your collateral value
3.  **satUSD is auto-staked** into the satUSD Staking Pool
4.  **You withdraw principal + yield + River Pts** after 45 days

No liquidation. No debt. Just yield.
