In crypto, distribution has always been a blunt instrument. Airdrops happen once, fast hands win, late hands lose. The result ? 

Liquidity churn, mercenary users, and misaligned communities.

River introduces something different: **Dynamic Airdrop Conversion** — a system where time itself becomes part of tokenomics.

### **How the Dynamic Airdrop Conversion Works**

River’s airdrop does not end in a single claim. Instead, it unfolds over 180 days through a **dynamic airdrop conversion system** that ties value to time.

At launch, 1B River Pts are allocated, representing up to 30% of total supply, equal to 30M RIVER.

Tokenomics → [https://docs.river.inc/tokenomics](https://docs.river.inc/tokenomics?ref=blog.river.inc)

Holders can convert their River Pts into $RIVER anytime during the 180-day window — in one transaction or multiple.

The key: **the longer you wait, the higher the rate.**

The difference is clear. Suppose you hold 1M River Pts:

If you convert within the first 24 hours of TGE, the amount of RIVER you receive will be close to zero.

-   Day 0, ~0 $RIVER
-   On Day 1, you receive 111 $RIVER
-   By Day 90, that becomes 10,000 $RIVER
-   By Day 180, the full potential is 30,000 $RIVER
