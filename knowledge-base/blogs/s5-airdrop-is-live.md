### What is River Pts

River Pts is the ecosystem points of River, issued in ERC-20 format. River Pts accrue based on your contributions: DeFi activity, River4FUN social campaigns, partner programs, and providing liquidity across supported chains.

At each seasonal airdrop, accumulated Pts convert to Staked RIVER through [Conversion 3.0.](https://blog.riverai.inc/dynamic-airdrop-conversion-3-0/)

River Pts contract address : `0xfc6be825925b7a83d131e33b46efef9084f0e014`

### Dynamic Airdrop Conversion 3.0

Dynamic Airdrop Conversion 3.0 is the first seasonal incentives model from River.

It changes how airdrops work, from a single distribution to a structure that runs every season. In a classic airdrop, the cycle ends after one claim.

Under Conversion 3.0, River Pts accrue through ecosystem activity each season, and you decide when to convert them to Staked RIVER. Contributors who stay active across seasons keep adding to what they earn, season after season.

Your conversion rate reflects two things

1.  Conversion density
2.  Staking epoch you choose

Longer commitments incur lower reshare penalties, resulting in better conversion rates and higher token output while minimizing deviation from the ideal conversion rate for other participants.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2026/07/image-8.png)

Conversion 3.0 in S6

Every staking epoch ends on a season boundary: January 1, April 1, July 1, or October 1. The first date lands on January 1, 2027.

[Season 6 is Live](https://blog.riverai.inc/season-6-is-live/) and River Pts are already accruing.

### How to Claim River Pts

-   Navigate to Airdrop [https://app.riverai.inc/airdrop](https://app.riverai.inc/airdrop?ref=blog.river.inc)
-   Connect your wallet
-   Connect your X
-   The page displays the eligible River Pts balance
-   Claim your River Pts before Sep 30.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2026/07/image-11.png)

S5 Airdrop - Claim

The claim window remains open until September 30 (when S6 ends). After which, S5 claims close permanently, and any unclaimed River Pts are forfeited.

#### Converting to Staked RIVER

Once claimed, River Pts sit in your wallet as ERC-20 tokens until you decide to convert.

-   Navigate to [airdrop page](https://app.riverai.inc/airdrop?ref=blog.riverai.inc)
-   Choose your Staking Epoch, 3 to 24 months
-   Confirm the conversion
-   Your staked RIVER position appears on the [RIVER page](https://app.riverai.inc/river?ref=blog.riverai.inc)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/a1/d6/a1d63116-fa30-4449-943c-8539b953740a/content/images/2026/07/image-12.png)

Staked RIVER

From there, the RIVER page displays your staked balance, epoch end date, and accrued weekly rewards. Convert any time before September 30, when S6 ends and the S5 Airdrop conversion window closes.
