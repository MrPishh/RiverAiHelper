In DeFi, one wrong price can trigger millions in liquidations within seconds. For River’s omni-CDP, where users can deposit on one chain and mint satUSD on another, precision is not optional. It is the foundation.

To make sure every collateral value is accurate, tamper-resistant, and available when it matters most, we have integrated Chainlink, the standard for onchain finance.

### Why Chainlink?

Chainlink leads the market in oracle security and reliability, securing [over $94B in Total Value Secured.](https://defillama.com/oracles/Chainlink?staking=true&liquidstaking=true&borrowed=true&ref=blog.river.inc)

It now accounts for about 68% of all oracle-secured value in DeFi. Its decentralized network delivers market-wide prices that DeFi depends on, with a track record proven in all market conditions.

### How River Uses It

River uses Chainlink price feeds on BOB, Arbitrum, Base, and BNB.

These feeds power our omni-CDP, keeping positions accurately collateralized and ensuring liquidity flows smoothly across chains.

### What It Means for You

-   **CDPs with accurate pricing**. Chainlink price feeds keep collateral values correct and prevent manipulation.
-   **Cross-chain liquidity with asset safety**. Chainlink pricing supports satUSD minting across chains while safeguarding collateral value.

By connecting River’s chain-abstraction stablecoin system with Chainlink’s proven infrastructure, we are giving users a safer and more reliable way to access liquidity across Web3.

### About River

River is building a chain-abstraction stablecoin system that connects assets, liquidity, and yield across multiple chains. Powered by the omni-CDP stablecoin satUSD, users can earn, leverage, and scale across ecosystems.

River features the first omni-CDP module that allows users to collateralize assets on Chain A and mint stablecoin satUSD on Chain B — without bridging the assets.
