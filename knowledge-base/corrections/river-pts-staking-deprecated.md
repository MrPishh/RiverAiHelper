# Correction: River Pts Staking ("Gold Staking Pool") — No Longer Generates Rewards

## What this was

During Season 3 (S3), River offered a mechanic for staking **already-earned, unconverted River Pts themselves** (not $RIVER, not satUSD) — called the "Gold Staking Pool," also referred to in places as "River Pts Staking." Users could stake, unstake, and claim raw River Pts to compound their balance before conversion. Documented (now outdated) in `blogs/river-s3-airdrop.md`.

## Current status — REMOVED

River Pts staking / the Gold Staking Pool no longer generates any additional rewards. Do NOT tell users that staking their (unconverted) River Pts will grow their balance or earn them more Pts.

**Important — the option may still be visible in the app UI.** Its continued visibility does NOT mean it is functional or reward-generating; the UI has not been updated to hide it. If a user asks about River Pts staking or the Gold Staking Pool:
- Confirm it was a valid, reward-generating mechanic during Season 3.
- Clarify that it no longer produces any additional rewards, even though the staking option may still appear in the app.
- Do NOT imply that staking River Pts today grows their balance or Pts total.

## Do not confuse with Staked $RIVER — that is still valid and current

This correction is ONLY about staking raw/unconverted **River Pts**. It does NOT apply to staking the **$RIVER token** (or satUSD) — those remain fully valid, active products:
- Staking $RIVER — live at app.riverai.inc/staking, earns River Pts weekly based on staking epoch and voting power
- Staking satUSD — live at app.riverai.inc/staking, earns satUSD+ (yield-bearing token)

The current, correct flow is: earn River Pts → convert them to Staked $RIVER via the seasonal Conversion mechanism (currently Conversion 3.0) → that Staked $RIVER continues to earn weekly River Pts rewards based on staking epoch/voting power. That ongoing $RIVER-staking-earns-Pts loop is real and current — only staking the raw, pre-conversion River Pts themselves is dead.
