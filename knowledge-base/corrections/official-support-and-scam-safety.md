# Official Support Policy and Scam Safety

## How to get official support

Official River support is **only** provided through support tickets created in this Discord server. Use the 🎫｜help-ticket channel to open a ticket and a staff member will assist you.

**Do not trust anyone offering help in the general or community channels.** These are public channels — anyone can post in them, including scammers posing as community members or admins. If you need help, always open a ticket.

Any support offered through:
- Direct messages (DMs) from any account
- External websites, apps, or links shared in the channel
- Other platforms (Telegram, X/Twitter, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Accounts claiming to be River admins or staff outside the official ticket system

...should be treated as a scam.

## Safety message — use this when someone asks if support or help they received is legitimate

If you're receiving help or support from someone outside this server, please be cautious — this is likely a scam.

A few important things to keep in mind:
• River admins and support staff will never DM you first or ask for your private keys, seed phrase, or login credentials
• You will never be asked to enter your seed phrase on any third-party website
• You will never be asked to connect your wallet to any site other than the official website: https://river.inc
• Anyone offering help in the general channel or community chat could be a scammer — public channels are not a safe place to receive support
• Always verify who you're speaking to and only use official links

Official River support is only available through tickets in this server. If you need help, open one here: <#1212718437164388392>

Do not proceed with anything until a staff member has confirmed it through the official ticket system.

## Red flags — signs you are being scammed

- Someone DMed you offering help after you posted in the server
- You were directed to an external website or app to "restore", "verify", or "sync" your wallet or dashboard
- You were asked to enter your seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key anywhere
- Support happened outside of the official Discord ticket system
- An "admin" or "moderator" contacted you first

If any of the above happened: do not proceed, do not click any links, and open an official support ticket immediately.
