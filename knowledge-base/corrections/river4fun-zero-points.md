# Correction: River4FUN Zero Points / Points Not Showing
Added: 2026-06-03

---

## Points Update Frequency

- **4FUN tweeting points** — updated **daily**
- **satUSD liquidity points** (except River staking) — updated **weekly** on the leaderboard page

Do NOT tell users that tweeting points update weekly — they update daily.

**When to mention frequency:** Only bring up the update frequency if the user seems confused about timing (e.g. "I posted yesterday and see nothing"). Mention it once only — do not repeat it later in the same response.

**When to mention satUSD:** Only if the user's question is specifically about satUSD or liquidity points. Do NOT mention satUSD for generic "my points aren't increasing" questions about Twitter/tweeting — it is not relevant and adds noise.

## Most Common Real Cause: Twitter Shadowban

The most frequent reason users see zero points despite posting every day is that Twitter has shadowbanned or limited their account. River has no control over Twitter's algorithm or what it flags as content farming or spamming.

### How to check for a shadowban

Using a different Twitter account, search the following under the **Latest** tab:

- For River4FUN tagging: `from:USERNAME @river4fun -filter:replies`
- For River protocol tagging: `from:USERNAME @riverdotinc -filter:replies`

Replace `USERNAME` with the user's Twitter handle. If their recent tweets don't appear in the results, they are shadowbanned.

Staff note: Users should always do this test themselves before opening a support ticket about missing points.

## Earning Points Without Twitter

Users can earn points from non-Twitter sources. Direct them to the S5 blog post for full details: <https://blog.riverai.inc/s5-live/>

## What NOT to Say

- Do NOT suggest the user reconnect their X account — if they are posting, it is already connected.
- Posts DO need to tag `@riverdotinc` or `@river4fun` to count — this requirement applies every season and has not changed.
- Do NOT say posts can instead tag a partner project in place of River — that alternative was old Season 1 behaviour only and no longer counts. Tagging River directly (`@riverdotinc` or `@river4fun`) is required regardless of season.
- Do NOT list staking as a requirement for earning River4FUN tweeting points. Staking (RIVER or satUSD) is a separate feature — it does not gate Twitter/X point eligibility.
- Do NOT volunteer the full setup checklist when a user asks a specific "is X enough?" question. Answer only what they asked. If they already have their wallet connected and are asking about tagging, confirm whether the tag format is correct and whether their X account is linked — nothing more.
- Do NOT bring up shadowban unless the user explicitly reports zero or missing points despite posting. A user asking "is that all I need?" has not reported a problem yet — do not pre-emptively troubleshoot.
