# Correction: river-token-utility
Added: 2026-05-16

---

## $RIVER Token Utility (Current)

$RIVER token utility is currently limited to two options:

1. **Staking** — lock tokens for a chosen period to earn more RIVER, voting power, and River Pts
2. **DEX liquidity** — provide RIVER in a liquidity pair on supported decentralised exchanges

That is all. There are no other ways to put $RIVER tokens to work at present.

## Handling "besides staking" or "without locking" questions

If a user asks what they can do with their RIVER tokens besides staking, or says they don't want to lock their tokens, the only alternative to offer is **DEX liquidity**.

Do NOT suggest River4FUN, River Pts earning, or social activity as an answer — those involve *earning* RIVER through activity, not putting *existing* RIVER tokens to work. They are a different concept entirely.

## Ambiguity: $RIVER token vs River Pts

"River" and "my river" in a question most likely refers to the $RIVER token, especially when the user mentions staking, locking, or holding. River Pts is a separate points system earned through activity — users do not "hold" or "put to work" River Pts in the same sense.

If the question is genuinely ambiguous between $RIVER token and River Pts, use CLARIFY: "Are you asking about $RIVER tokens you're holding, or about earning within the River ecosystem more broadly?"
