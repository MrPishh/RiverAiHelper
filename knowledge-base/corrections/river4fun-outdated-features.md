# Correction: River4FUN — Staking and Voting No Longer Available

## Staking tokens on River4FUN — REMOVED

Staking of third-party tokens (wBNB, USD1, and other BNB chain tokens) on the River4FUN platform is **no longer available**. Any docs or help guides describing this feature are outdated.

**What IS available for staking:**
- Staking $RIVER tokens — live at app.river.inc/staking
- Staking satUSD — live at app.river.inc/staking (earns satUSD+, a yield-bearing token)

Do NOT tell users they can stake wBNB, USD1, or other tokens on the River4FUN app to earn River Pts. That feature has been removed.

---

## Voting for projects using River Points — REMOVED

Voting for projects or creators using River Points (Pts) is **no longer available**. The old system where staked River Pts counted as votes (1 Pt = 1 vote) on the 4FUN platform has been discontinued.

**Current voting mechanism:**
- Voting is now based on the **$RIVER token**, not River Points.
- Do NOT tell users they can vote for projects or creators using River Pts.

---

## River Pts Vault / "Locks until TGE" — OUTDATED

Any reference to a River Pts Vault that "locks until TGE" or "converts directly into $RIVER at TGE" is outdated. TGE already happened on September 22, 2025. Do not tell users their Pts will lock until TGE — that language no longer applies.

---

## Season-specific reward percentages — DO NOT QUOTE

Do not quote reward breakdowns or quest percentages from past seasons (e.g. "10% of Season 2 rewards come from quests"). Season 2 ended long ago — we are currently in Season 5. Past season mechanics are not current. If a user asks about current reward structure, direct them to check the official app or open a ticket for up-to-date figures.

---

## What River4FUN still does

River4FUN is the social contribution layer of River. Users can:
- Connect their X (Twitter) account
- Post about River or supported partner projects
- Earn River Points based on impressions, engagement quality, and consistency
- River Pts convert to $RIVER via the seasonal Conversion mechanism
