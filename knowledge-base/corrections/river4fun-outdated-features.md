# Correction: River4FUN — Staking and Voting No Longer Available

## Staking tokens on River4FUN — REMOVED

Staking of third-party tokens (wBNB, USD1, and other BNB chain tokens) on the River4FUN platform is **no longer available**. Any docs or help guides describing this feature are outdated.

**What IS available for staking:**
- Staking $RIVER tokens — live at app.riverai.inc/staking
- Staking satUSD — live at app.riverai.inc/staking (earns satUSD+, a yield-bearing token)

Do NOT tell users they can stake wBNB, USD1, or other tokens on the River4FUN app to earn River Pts. That feature has been removed.

**Specific tokens that were previously stakeable — now removed:** This was never a single fixed list published anywhere — River4FUN accepted **trending tokens on BNB Chain**, and the eligible roster rotated and grew over the feature's lifetime (roughly on the order of ~30 different tokens total across that period, not just one or two). Confirmed named examples from official River materials: Mubarak, TST, wBNB, USD1 — these are the only names we have documented sources for; many others existed but aren't individually recorded in the knowledge base. If a user asks about staking any BNB Chain token on River4FUN — Mubarak, TST, wBNB, USD1, or **any other token name not listed here** — including someone referencing an old screenshot or guide: confirm it was plausible and likely real at the time (the mechanic accepted a rotating set of trending BNB Chain tokens, not just the few names documented here), but the entire feature has since been removed and staking any token on River4FUN no longer works or exists in the app today. Do not say "no, that token was never supported" just because the specific name isn't recognized — treat any such question as referring to this same removed feature.

**River4FUN has ONE active track, not two.** When answering "how do I earn with River4FUN," describe ONLY the Social Engagement Pool (connect X, post, earn Pts based on impressions/engagement/consistency). Do NOT invent or describe a second "Staking Pool" track as part of River4FUN — even though $RIVER and satUSD staking are real products that earn River Pts, they are separate products (at app.riverai.inc/staking), not a River4FUN participation track. Do not merge the two into "two participation tracks."

## Voting for projects using River Points — REMOVED

Voting for projects or creators using River Points (Pts) is **no longer available**. The old system where staked River Pts counted as votes (1 Pt = 1 vote) on the 4FUN platform has been discontinued.

**Current voting mechanism:**
- Voting is now based on the **$RIVER token**, not River Points.
- Do NOT tell users they can vote for projects or creators using River Pts.

## What River4FUN still does

River4FUN is the social contribution layer of River. Users can:
- Connect their X (Twitter) account
- Post about River or supported partner projects
- Earn River Points based on impressions, engagement quality, and consistency
- River Pts convert to $RIVER via the seasonal Conversion mechanism
